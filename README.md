# 📊 Global Importers Sales & Profitability Dashboard (Power BI)

An advanced Business Intelligence and financial analytics solution built to solve critical operational and revenue challenges for Global Importers, transforming raw, fragmented sales data into strategic, decision-ready insights.

## 💼 Business Problem & Solution

### 🛑 The Business Challenge:
Global Importers faced critical blind spots in their sales operations:
- **Lack of Visibility on Profit Margins:** Management couldn't accurately track which product categories or regions were driving actual profitability versus those draining resources due to high costs.
- **Disconnected Sales Trends:** Difficulty in identifying the root causes behind revenue shifts and sales volume fluctuations across years.
- **Operational Inefficiencies:** Sales teams lacked a centralized tool to filter performance by salesperson, customer tiers, and specific timeframes, leading to delayed decision-making.

### ✅ The Data-Driven Solution:
- Developed an end-to-end interactive Power BI dashboard utilizing **Power Query** for robust ETL pipelines and relational data modeling.
- Integrated financial tracking metrics (**Total Cost, Profitability %, Net Profit**) directly linked with sales volume (`20M` total sales, `1M+` units) to expose hidden cost drivers.
- Implemented dynamic, multi-level slicers (Fiscal Year, Quarter, Month, Customer, Product, Salesperson) empowering stakeholders to drill down into root causes instantly.
